# LinkedIn Featured Section Descriptions

## Commodity Hedge Risk Dashboard

Power BI-ready commodity risk analytics project using synthetic Canadian grain company data. Tracks physical exposure, futures hedges, net open exposure, hedge ratio, P&L, basis risk, margin, VaR approximation, stress testing, counterparty exposure, and executive risk flags.

## Drilling Project Controls Dashboard

Power BI-ready energy operations dashboard using synthetic drilling data. Tracks AFE budget, actual cost, cost variance, schedule variance, drilling progress, NPT, rig utilization, contractor performance, HSE indicators, forecast final cost, and risk flags.

## Insurance Loss Ratio Dashboard

Power BI-ready group insurance analytics project using synthetic benefits finance data. Tracks earned premium, claims, loss ratio, claims frequency, severity, profitability, product mix, high-risk employers, and renewal risk.

## Pension Investment Risk Dashboard

Power BI-ready investment risk analytics project using synthetic retirement portfolio data. Tracks allocation, portfolio value, returns, benchmark comparison, active return, volatility, drawdown, FX exposure, contribution flows, retirement projection, and scenario impacts.
