The Python and pandas workflow calculates monthly loss ratio, policy profitability, claims frequency, claims severity, renewal risk flags, employer summary, product mix, and executive KPIs.

The Power BI-ready package includes CSV outputs, SQLite schema, SQL views, DAX measures, build guide, README, screenshot instructions, and an interview demo script.
