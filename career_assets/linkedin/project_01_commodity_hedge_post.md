## More Technical LinkedIn Post

I recently built a synthetic Commodity Hedge Risk Dashboard to practice end-to-end risk analytics for a Canadian grain company scenario.

The business problem: a company buys and sells physical grain while using futures contracts to hedge commodity price exposure. The risk team needs daily visibility into open exposure, hedge ratio, P&L, basis risk, margin requirements, stress test impact, and counterparty concentration.

Technical workflow:

- Python and pandas synthetic data generation
- CSV outputs for Power BI import
- SQLite schema and SQL views
- Daily risk metric calculations
- Counterparty exposure and credit utilization logic
- VaR 95/99 approximation using rolling P&L volatility
- Stress scenarios for commodity price, basis, and FX shocks
- DAX measure documentation for Power BI

The project includes raw tables, analytical tables, SQL scripts, a Power BI build guide, DAX measures, dashboard design documentation, and interview talking points.

All data is synthetic, but the structure is realistic enough to demonstrate risk reporting, dashboard design, and business analysis skills for Financial Data Analyst, Risk Reporting Analyst, BI Analyst, Commodity Risk Analyst, and Treasury/Risk Reporting roles.
