# Insurance Loss Ratio Interview Questions

## Questions to Practice

- What is loss ratio?
- How do frequency and severity explain claims experience?
- Why should premium and claims be normalized by member count?
- How would renewal risk be used by a finance or underwriting team?
- What are the limits of this synthetic model?

## Short Answer Themes

- Loss ratio is incurred claims divided by earned premium.
- High frequency and high severity require different business responses.
- Renewal risk flags are directional and not a replacement for actuarial pricing.
