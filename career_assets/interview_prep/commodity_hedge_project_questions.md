# Commodity Hedge Project Interview Questions

## Questions to Practice

- What business problem does the commodity hedge dashboard solve?
- How did you model physical exposure and futures exposure?
- What does hedge ratio mean in this dashboard?
- How did you approximate P&L and VaR?
- What is basis risk, and why does it matter?
- How would you improve this project in a production environment?

## Short Answer Themes

- The project helps risk teams monitor hedge alignment, residual exposure, margin pressure, and counterparty concentration.
- VaR is simplified and uses rolling P&L volatility, not a full production market risk engine.
- The dashboard is executive-facing and designed for decision support.
