# Project 4 Resume Bullets - Pension Investment Risk Dashboard

## Investment Risk Analyst Resume

- Built a synthetic pension investment risk dashboard package using Python, SQL, pandas, SQLite, and Power BI-ready outputs to monitor allocation, returns, benchmarks, volatility, drawdown, FX exposure, contributions, and scenarios.
- Calculated portfolio daily returns, cumulative returns, active returns, maximum drawdown, annualized volatility, allocation drift, and stress scenario impact.

## Financial Data Analyst Resume

- Modeled retirement portfolios, members, holdings, daily prices, FX rates, contributions, withdrawals, and benchmarks to support investment reporting and member outcome analysis.
- Prepared executive dashboard documentation for portfolio value, allocation, performance, risk profile, FX exposure, and retirement projection metrics.

## BI Analyst Resume

- Created DAX measures and a six-page Power BI build guide for Executive Summary, Asset Allocation, Returns vs Benchmark, Volatility & Drawdown, FX Exposure & Scenarios, and Retirement Projection.
