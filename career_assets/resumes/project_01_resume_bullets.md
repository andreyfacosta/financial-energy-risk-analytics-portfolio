# Project 1 Resume Bullets - Commodity Hedge Risk Dashboard

## Financial Data Analyst Resume

- Built a synthetic commodity hedge risk analytics project using Python, pandas, SQL, and Power BI-ready CSV outputs to model physical exposure, futures exposure, P&L, margin requirements, and executive risk KPIs.
- Developed a reproducible data pipeline that generated synthetic financial and commodity datasets, calculated risk metrics, and produced dashboard-ready analytical tables for Power BI reporting.
- Created executive reporting documentation for net open exposure, hedge ratio, daily and cumulative P&L, VaR approximation, stress testing, and counterparty credit utilization.

## Risk Reporting Analyst Resume

- Designed a commodity risk reporting workflow using Python, SQL, and Power BI documentation to monitor hedge exposure, net open exposure, basis risk, margin pressure, VaR approximation, stress test impact, and risk flags.
- Implemented synthetic risk metrics for physical grain positions and futures hedges, including hedge ratio, P&L movement, margin requirement, counterparty exposure, and high-risk exception reporting.
- Prepared DAX measures and dashboard specifications for executive risk review, supporting clear escalation of over-hedged positions, large net exposures, high VaR, and counterparty limit pressure.

## BI Analyst Resume

- Created a Power BI-ready commodity hedge dashboard package with synthetic CSV datasets, SQLite schema, SQL views, DAX measures, relationship model documentation, and a six-page report build guide.
- Modeled dimension and fact tables for calendar, commodities, counterparties, physical transactions, futures positions, daily risk metrics, risk flags, stress tests, and counterparty exposure.
- Documented dashboard pages for executive summary, exposure and hedge ratio, P&L and margin, basis risk, stress testing, and counterparty/region analysis.

## Commodity Risk Analyst Resume

- Built a commodity hedge exposure model for a synthetic Canadian grain company scenario, calculating physical exposure, futures hedge exposure, net open exposure, hedge ratio, basis risk, P&L, and margin requirements.
- Developed stress testing and VaR approximation outputs to evaluate commodity price, basis, and FX shocks across multiple grain commodities and futures contract months.
- Produced counterparty exposure analysis with credit utilization and risk flags to support management review of watch list and limit-pressure counterparties.
