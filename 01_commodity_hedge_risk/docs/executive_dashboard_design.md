# Executive Dashboard Design

## Audience

- CFO
- Treasurer
- Risk manager
- Commodity trading leadership
- FP&A / finance business partner

## Executive Question

Are our physical grain exposures appropriately hedged, and where are P&L, basis, margin, stress, or counterparty risks becoming material?

## Page 1: Executive Summary

Top KPI cards:

- Net Open Exposure CAD
- Hedge Ratio
- Daily P&L CAD
- Cumulative P&L CAD
- Margin Requirement CAD
- VaR 99 CAD
- Open Risk Flags

Main visuals:

- Net open exposure by commodity.
- Cumulative P&L trend.
- Top risk flags table.
- Counterparty credit utilization table.

Decision supported:

- Decide which commodities need hedge adjustment or management review.

## Page 2: Exposure & Hedge Ratio

Main visuals:

- Physical vs futures exposure trend.
- Hedge ratio by commodity and date.
- Net open exposure matrix by commodity and contract month.
- Slicer for region and commodity.

Decision supported:

- Identify under-hedged and over-hedged exposures.

## Page 3: P&L and Margin

Main visuals:

- Daily P&L by commodity.
- Cumulative P&L trend.
- Margin requirement by commodity.
- Futures position summary.

Decision supported:

- Understand mark-to-market movement and collateral pressure.

## Page 4: Basis Risk

Main visuals:

- Cash vs futures price trend.
- Basis CAD/MT by commodity.
- Basis risk CAD by commodity.
- Regional basis comparison.

Decision supported:

- Identify regions or commodities where local cash prices diverge from futures.

## Page 5: Stress Testing

Main visuals:

- Scenario impact by commodity.
- Total scenario impact.
- Heatmap by commodity and scenario.

Decision supported:

- Estimate downside under price, FX, and basis shocks.

## Page 6: Counterparty / Region View

Main visuals:

- Counterparty exposure vs credit limit.
- Region exposure chart.
- Credit rating distribution.
- Counterparty risk flags.

Decision supported:

- Prioritize counterparty follow-up and credit review.

## Recruiter Talking Point

This dashboard demonstrates the ability to translate a business risk problem into a data model, KPIs, SQL logic, Python analysis, Power BI measures, and executive-ready reporting.
