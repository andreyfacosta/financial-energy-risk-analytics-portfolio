# Data Dictionary

## physical_transactions

Physical grain purchase and sale contracts.

- `transaction_id`: Unique synthetic transaction identifier.
- `trade_date`: Date the physical transaction was agreed.
- `delivery_start`: Start of delivery exposure window.
- `delivery_end`: End of delivery exposure window.
- `commodity_id`: Commodity key.
- `counterparty_id`: Counterparty key.
- `region`: Canadian region.
- `buy_sell`: Buy or Sell.
- `quantity_mt`: Quantity in metric tonnes.
- `cash_price_cad_mt`: Cash price in CAD per metric tonne.
- `fx_exposure_usd`: Approximate USD exposure portion.
- `status`: Open or Delivered.

## futures_positions

Futures hedge positions.

- `position_id`: Unique synthetic futures position identifier.
- `trade_date`: Date position was opened.
- `contract_month`: Futures contract month.
- `commodity_id`: Commodity key.
- `hedge_type`: Inventory, forward sale, or basis hedge.
- `exchange`: Exchange.
- `buy_sell`: Buy or Sell.
- `contracts`: Number of futures contracts.
- `contract_size_mt`: Contract size in metric tonnes.
- `futures_price_usd_mt`: Futures price in USD per metric tonne.
- `broker`: Clearing broker.
- `initial_margin_usd_per_contract`: Initial margin per contract.
- `status`: Open or Closed.

## daily_risk_metrics

Power BI-ready analytical output generated by `02_analyze_hedge_risk.py`.

- `physical_exposure_cad`: Signed physical exposure.
- `futures_exposure_cad`: Signed futures exposure.
- `net_open_exposure_cad`: Physical plus futures exposure.
- `hedge_ratio`: Absolute futures quantity divided by absolute physical quantity.
- `daily_pnl_cad`: Approximate daily mark-to-market P&L.
- `cumulative_pnl_cad`: Cumulative P&L by commodity.
- `basis_cad_mt`: Average cash price minus futures price.
- `basis_risk_cad`: Rolling basis volatility multiplied by open physical quantity.
- `margin_requirement_cad`: Estimated active futures initial margin.
- `var_95_cad`: Approximate 95 percent VaR from rolling P&L volatility.
- `var_99_cad`: Approximate 99 percent VaR from rolling P&L volatility.
